Abs close the session, they don't open it. Hanging Leg Raise ends Monday, Cable Crunch ends Thursday. Want more, add a set to any day. They show up when the fat drops, not from extra crunches.

Progression

where muscle is built

Add reps before weight. Take one set up a rep each week until every set sits at the top of the range, then add 2.5kg and start again. Bench, top of a 4×5–6:

Week 1
95×595×595×595×5
Week 2
95×695×595×595×5
Week 3
95×695×695×595×5
Week 4
95×695×695×695×6
+
All four sets at the top, so next week is 97.5kg × 5 and it repeats. Same rule on your 3-set lifts.
Holding it together
  • Defend, don't chase. In a deficit, matching last week is muscle staying.
  • Log every set, weight, reps, RIR. You can't defend a number you don't track.
  • Deload week 5 and 9, or sooner if you stall hard. Cut sets ~40%, keep it light.
Injury & Cramp Prevention
  • Dynamic Warm-Ups: Run 5-10 min light cardio followed by move-specific warm-up sets. Avoid pre-workout static stretching.
  • Straps & Gear: Deploy straps on heavy pulls (RDLs, Rows) when grip fatigue risks spinal alignment.
  • Crush Cramps: Hit your 3.5-4.0L fluid target daily and supplement clean electrolytes (Magnesium/Sodium).
  • Controlled Eccentrics: Control the lowering phase completely. Never bounce heavy loads off your joints.

What this pace means

2,365 a day is about 535 under your maintenance, roughly 0.5kg a week. Sustainable, and 240g protein is more than enough to hold your muscle. The trade-off: a true 12% lands closer to mid-September than end of August at this intake every day. Want August back? Keep 250g carbs on the five training days but drop Wednesday and Sunday carbs, the weekly average falls and the rate climbs. Your call.

  • 240g protein is the one number you never miss. Train or rest, it holds your 71kg of muscle through the deficit.
  • 45g fat is the floor, not a target to cut under. Keep it there for hormones.
  • Cluster carbs around the five sessions and the Saturday meal out. Rest days come in lighter on their own.

How this is worked out

Target weight
target = lean ÷ (1 − 0.12)
Holds your lean mass and solves for the weight that puts you at 12%. 74.8 ÷ 0.88 ≈ 85kg. It recomputes from your newest scan, so building muscle lifts the target with you.
7-day average & trend
avg = mean(last 7 weigh-ins)
trend = slope(weight vs day) × 7
One morning swings a kilo on water. The average strips that out, the slope gives a fair kg-per-week even with gaps. Aim for 0.4–0.8.
Composition verdict
fat ↓ & muscle held → clean loss
fat ↓ & muscle ↓ >0.4 → ease deficit
fat ↑ otherwise → review intake
Runs each new scan against the one before. Steer daily, verify monthly.
