The workout

tick as you go
Abs · after lifting

Abs close the session, they don't open it. Hanging Leg Raise ends Monday, Cable Crunch ends Thursday. Want more, add a set to any day. They show up when the fat drops, not from extra crunches.

Warm up first

before the heavy work
  • 5 min easy on the bike or treadmill to raise the temperature.
  • Ramp the first big lift: empty bar, ~50%, ~70%, ~85% for a few reps each. Never hit a top set cold.
  • Lower: leg swings, bodyweight squats, glute bridges, hip 90/90, ankle rocks.
  • Upper: band pull-aparts, shoulder dislocates, scapular push-ups, a light first set.
Cramps · your leg-press one
A lying leg press cramping your abs is the hip flexor and lower abs seizing fully shortened: knees to chest, pelvis tucked, braced hard. A breath-hold and a deficit tip it over.
Knees out of your chest, pelvis down
Breathe out as you press. Salt before training, 300–400mg magnesium at night, keep carbs on lifting days. If it cramps, straighten the leg, breathe out, ease off.
Bracing & breathing
Breathe into the belly and brace like you're taking a punch. Heavy low reps: hold at the top, exhale through the sticking point. Higher reps: breathe normally, exhale on the push. A full breath-hold through long sets is what makes you light-headed.
Control & range
Most pulls are form breaking under fatigue or too much weight, so keep 1–2 in the tank on compounds. Lower under control, the fast drop is where strains happen. Full but pain-free range. Sharp pain is a stop signal, burn is not.
Straps & gear
Straps when grip gives out first: RDLs, rows, pulldowns, shrugs. Belt for top sets of squat, hip thrust, overhead press. Wrist wraps for heavy press. Knee sleeves for warmth. Flat stable shoes on lower days.

Progression

where muscle is built

What this pace means

2,365 a day is about 535 under maintenance, roughly 0.5kg a week. Sustainable, and 240g protein holds your muscle. The trade-off: a true 12% lands closer to mid-September than end of August. Want August back? Keep 250g carbs on training days but drop Wednesday and Sunday carbs, the weekly average falls. Your call.

  • 240g protein is the one number you never miss. Train or rest, it holds your 71kg of muscle through the deficit.
  • 45g fat is the floor, not a target to cut under. Keep it there for hormones.
  • Cluster carbs around the five sessions and the Saturday meal out. Rest days come in lighter on their own.

How this is worked out

Target weight
target = lean ÷ (1 − 0.12)
Holds your lean mass and solves for 12%. 74.8 ÷ 0.88 ≈ 85kg. Recomputes from your newest scan, so building muscle lifts the target.
7-day average & trend
avg = mean(last 7) · trend = slope × 7
One morning swings a kilo on water. The average strips that out, the slope gives a fair kg/week. Aim for 0.4–0.8.
