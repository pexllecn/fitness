How to read RPE

RPE = reps left in the tank when you stop. Aim for RPE 8: about two good reps still in you. No training to failure on a cut.

Method & Algorithms

no black boxes
Two engines run side by side. Daily weigh-ins drive the trend and projection, because one reading is noisy (water, salt, food) but the average is honest. Monthly scans drive the composition read, the only way to separate fat from muscle. Each is used for what it is good at.

Fat mass & lean mass

fat mass = weight × bodyfat% ÷ 100
lean (FFM) = weight − fat mass

Every composition number builds on these two. From your May scan: 92.6 × 19.2 ÷ 100 = 17.8kg fat, leaving 74.8kg lean.

Target weight

target = latest lean ÷ (1 − 0.12)

Holds your lean mass and solves for the weight at 12% body fat. 74.8 ÷ 0.88 = ~85kg. It recomputes from your newest scan, so if you build muscle the target rises with you.

7-day average

avg = mean( last 7 daily weights )

A single morning can swing ±1kg on water alone. The average strips that noise out. Act on the average, never one day.

Weekly trend

trend = slope( weight vs day ) × 7

A least-squares line through your last few weeks of entries, so irregular logging still gives a fair kg-per-week rate. Healthy cut band is 0.4–0.8 kg/wk.

Goal ETA

weeks = (7-day avg − target) ÷ |weekly loss|

Projects when you reach ~85kg at the current rate. It needs roughly two weeks of daily weights and an actual downward trend before it will show a date.

Composition verdict (decision tree)

fat ↓ & muscle held → clean fat loss
fat ↓ & muscle ↓ >0.4kg → ease deficit +150
fat ↑ & muscle ↑ ≥ fat → lean gain
fat ↑ otherwise → review intake
fat flat (±0.15kg) → holding, trust avg

Run automatically on each new scan against the one before it.

Rate guardrails

> 0.8 kg/wk → add 150 kcal (protect muscle)
< 0.4 kg/wk for 2 wks → cut 150 or add cardio

Why both, not one

Daily-only can't tell fat loss from muscle loss. Monthly-only is too sparse to steer week to week, and one scan carries its own error. Layering them is the dependable choice: steer daily, verify monthly.
