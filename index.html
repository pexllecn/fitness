How to read RPE

RPE = reps left in the tank when you stop. Aim for RPE 8: about two good reps still in you. No training to failure on a cut.

Deloads · weeks 5 & 9

Rest day & refeed

Rest day

Skip the pre-gym oats, keep that whey scoop in water, drop lunch rice to 60g. Lands ~2,050, protein still ~190g.

Refeed · every 2–3 wks

One day at ~2,900: carbs up ~150g, fat low, protein held. The controlled blowout. No free days.

Cardio & steps

Daily

  • 10–12k steps, the fat-loss engine.
  • Walk calls, take stairs, 15-min lunch walk banks ~2k.

Wed + Sat

  • 2 × 25–30 min incline walk or easy bike.
  • No HIIT, it steals leg recovery. Add a 3rd only if the scale stalls.

Scale rules

  • Weigh daily, morning, after the toilet, before food.
  • Act only on the 7-day average.
  • Faster than 0.8 kg/wk? Add 150 kcal, that pace eats muscle.
  • Slower than 0.4 kg/wk after 2 weeks? Cut 150 or add cardio.
  • Ignore the scale 2–3 days after a high day.
