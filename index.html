How to read RPE — read first

RPE = reps left in the tank when you stop. Aim for RPE 8: stop with about two good reps still in you. No training to failure on a cut.

Example — Back Squat

Card says 4 × 4–6. Warm up, then four sets at 100kg.

  • The last set is your gauge: ~2 reps left = RPE 8 = perfect.
  • 4+ left, flew up = too light → 105kg next time.
  • Couldn't hit 4, or last rep a grind = too heavy → drop ~5kg.

Progression

Double progression: hit the top of the range on all sets at RPE 8, then add 2.5kg each side and reset to the bottom. Log every set.

Deloads — weeks 5 & 9

Cut sets ~40%, drop loads ~10%, keep RPE 5–6, pair with a refeed. If the elbow niggles before then, deload pressing early. Never train through tendon pain.

Cardio & Recovery

Steps are the engine, muscle-sparing. No HIIT, it steals leg recovery you can't spare. The food makes the deficit; cardio is the dial you turn only if the scale stalls.

Cardio & steps

Daily

  • 10,000–12,000 steps. You already hit ~9.7k on a quiet day.
  • Walk your calls, take stairs, a 15-min lunch walk banks ~2k.

Wed + Sat

  • 2 × 25–30 min incline walk or easy bike, conversational.
  • Only add a third if the average flatlines 2 weeks, before cutting food.

Recovery

Sleep — non-negotiable

  • 7.5–8h, fixed wake time, weekends too.
  • Last caffeine early afternoon, keep pre-gym dose small.
  • Bad sleep drives the next-day blowout. Protect it.

Supplements

  • Creatine 5g daily, no loading.
  • Vitamin D3, sensible at Irish latitude.
  • Fish oil for joints and the elbow.
  • Check halal capsules. No fat burners.

Hydration

  • 3 litres+ daily, protein and creatine need it.
  • Hunger is often thirst, drink first.

Daily shape

  • Pre-log each morning, copy yesterday.
  • Train evening with Mufleh.
  • Lunch walk, screens off before bed.
Lock-In & Arc

Your block was never knowledge, it was execution drifting once novelty fades. This runs on systems, not motivation. Motivation's gone by week three. These won't be.

The lock-in system

  1. Bolt it to the gym anchor. You never skip Mufleh. Tell him the target, send him your Sunday weigh-in average weekly. Make the diet as accountable as the lifting.
  2. Sunday checkpoint, 10 min. Log the 7-day average, one progress photo in the same light, adjust only if the rules say so.
  3. Pre-commit to the stall now. Around week 4–6 the scale freezes while fat still leaves. Decision made today: do nothing, hold.
  4. Judge by data, not the mirror. Trunk fat empties last. Trust the average.

Scale rules

  • Weigh daily, morning, after the toilet, before food.
  • Act only on the 7-day average.
  • Faster than 0.8 kg/wk? Add 150 kcal, that pace eats muscle.
  • Slower than 0.4 kg/wk after 2 weeks? Cut 150 or add cardio.
  • Ignore the scale 2–3 days after a high day.

The 12-week arc

Wk 1–4 · Establish
Lock the routine. ~0.6–0.7kg/wk, first whoosh after a flat patch. ~90kg by week 4.
Wk 5 · Deload + refeed
Back off, one refeed. You'll look fuller, that's glycogen. Don't panic.
Wk 6–8 · The grind
Novelty's gone, the system earns its keep. Scale may stall, hold. Arms and face leaner. ~87kg.
Wk 9 · Deload + re-scan
Re-Tanita to confirm fat's leaving, lean's holding. Adjust off real data.
Wk 10–12 · Final push
Tighten to ~84–85kg / 12%. Top abs visible. Late-August target hit.
After · Decide
Hold it, or push to ~10% for a full shred into September.
